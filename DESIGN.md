# Design

Mode: Operate / Read. A Chinese-first thinking workspace for conversation, file reading and visual creation. Default daylight palette: soft mineral white, green ink and muted olive. The conversation and composer take priority; no marketing hero, gradient, oversized decoration or icon-tile grid.

Desktop: 254px sidebar, persistent workspace header, centered reading column and anchored composer. Mobile: overlay navigation, 44px primary controls, scrollable conversation, full-width composer. Light and dark palettes share semantic variables. Icons use a single authored SVG stroke family. Body text uses the user's Chinese UI fonts; code uses monospace. Reduced motion is respected. Authentication is a focused dialog. Guest/privacy, saving failure and quota states remain visible.

Identity: an asymmetric, two-ribbon V, cut by a narrow negative-space seam. The rising right stroke suggests an idea moving forward; the two tones echo dialogue. The mark uses a 64-unit grid, green ink #294c3e, paper #f2f5e9 and sage #c4d7a0. The custom geometric VEAIX wordmark is drawn as vector paths, with consistent stroke weight and optical spacing. Keep the mark square; do not stretch it or add gradients. Assets: logo-mark.svg, logo-wordmark.svg and favicon.svg. The same mark appears in navigation and login.

Model selection is a compact anchored panel, not an operating-system select menu. Names and short purposes form a two-line hierarchy; selected states, vision support and paid access are explicit. Arrow keys, Home/End, Enter and Escape are supported. The panel scrolls within the viewport on small screens. All message actions share inline-flex alignment, 20px line height, equal padding and consistent SVG icons; downloads and regeneration therefore share a baseline and hit area.
